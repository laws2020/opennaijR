1. What opennaijR Is

opennaijR is a software package that provides structured, reproducible, and programmatic access to publicly available African economic and financial data.

opennaijR does not host, store, resell, or redistribute government data. All data accessed through opennaijR remains the property of the respective originating institutions. When you call cbn("inflation"), your R session connects directly to the Central Bank of Nigeria’s data infrastructure. The data flows to you from the institution – opennaijR provides the automation logic that makes that connection clean and reproducible.

2. What the Software Provides

opennaijR provides:

  • Automation tools that reduce manual data preparation work
  • Structured connectors to official institutional endpoints
  • Data normalisation and transformation logic (cleaning, date parsing, column naming)
  • Local caching and workflow integration utilities

Any fees charged in the future apply strictly to use of the opennaijR software infrastructure and engineering services. They do not grant ownership of, or exclusive access to, the underlying public data, which remains free at its source.
